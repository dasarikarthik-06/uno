# UNO Game: Version 2

# Add following new cards in the game

## Skip
- If a player plays this card, the next player turn will be skipped and can't play
  for one turn
- If there are only 2 players the turn again comes back to the player who played 
  the card
- This card is available in all color
- They can play this card only if the color is same or the previous card is also skip

## +2
- If a player plays this card, the next player receives two cards from the deck
  automatically and their chances will be skipped.
- If the next player also has the same card, the 2nd next player has to draw 2 + 2 
  card from the deck, and it keeps repeating until the next player has +2 card
- If there are only 2 players the turn again comes back to the player who played 
  the card
- This card is available in all color
- They can play this card only if the color is same or the previous card is also skip

Both of the above cards now can go to player hand in the initial distribution or
can come from deck as well
